# Noise Test Log

**Open:** double-click `Noise Test Log.url`, or go to https://claude.ai/artifact/1t3cQum1bYyprLD4k2Mi5X and sign in to claude.ai.
**Access:** André Knoesen shares the page with you as a Contributor. Until then the link does not open.

The app is the record of the pre-campaign noise tests (M0 to M6). Spectra stay in Box; the app records what each file is and the conditions it was taken under.

| Tab | Use |
|---|---|
| **Measurements** | One tab per measurement: purpose, conditions, steps, frames still to take, the logging form, the done-when, and every file logged for it |
| **Decision** | P_op, frame time, frames per acquisition and argon frames, checked against the 30-minute window; André signs off here |
| **Full log & export** | Every entry and event, with CSV export of `run_log.csv` and `events.csv` |

**Quick log bar** (top of every page): Laser on, Laser off, Shutter open, Shutter closed, Coupling adjusted, Half-wave plate changed, Ophir reading, Note, App feedback. One tap, time-stamped.

**App feedback** (last button in the quick log bar): tap it whenever something in the app is awkward, confusing or missing. Use the app as it is during a run; it is updated after each day's run, never during one.

**If the connection drops** (winery Wi-Fi): the header shows *Not connected*, entries are kept on that device, and they upload automatically the next time the page connects. Use the same device until the pending count in the header clears.

Instructions for the measurements themselves are in `Communications and docs\README_START_HERE.md`.
